01 / AI INTERVIEW COACH Completed

MockAI

What it doesPractise job interviews and get scored the way a real interviewer would. You record your answers, and MockAI rates what you said, how clearly you said it and how confident you looked, then explains every score.
ResultThree AI models work together to produce one clear, explainable report for every answer. Completed as my final-year project at COMSATS.
How it's builtDistilBERT judges answer quality, speech-to-text measures delivery and facial-expression analysis reads composure, combined 50/30/20. FastAPI and MongoDB backend with separate candidate and admin apps.
React.js → FastAPI → FFmpeg → DistilBERT · Speech-to-Text · FERPlus → Late fusion
Python FastAPI React.js MongoDB DistilBERT FERPlus FFmpeg

03 / AI TRAVEL PLATFORM Client Project

FlightOne

What it doesBook a trip by simply typing what you want. Tell Ava, the AI travel assistant, "Lahore to Karachi on 10 Oct" and she shows the best options, takes you through booking and stays with you after you've paid.

06 / AI DOCUMENT ASSISTANT IN DEVELOPMENT

Intelligence OS

What it doesAsk questions about your company's own documents and get answers with the exact page they came from. Each organisation's data stays completely separate.
ResultIn a 50-question test it finds the right source in its top 5 results 97% of the time, and correctly says "I don't know" whenever the answer isn't in the documents. Every update must pass this test before release.
