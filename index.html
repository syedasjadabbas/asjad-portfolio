01 / AI System Completed

MockAI

ChallengeEvaluate interview performance across answer content, speech and facial expression at the same time, without manual review.
SolutionA multimodal pipeline that scores each recorded answer on content (DistilBERT semantic scoring), delivery (Google Speech-to-Text with pace, pause and filler analysis) and composure (YuNet face detection with FERPlus emotion analysis). A late-fusion engine weights the three 50 / 30 / 20 into an explainable report. Built with separate candidate and admin platforms on a FastAPI and MongoDB backend.

04 / Hospitality Platform Built & Tested

Serene Management

ChallengeRun daily hotel operations for one or several properties without staff seeing another property's data or billing records ending up inconsistent.
SolutionA hotel property management system covering reservations, guest profiles, rooms, housekeeping, rates, billing, reporting and night audit. Permissions are enforced on the server, each property's data is isolated, billing and night audit run inside database transactions, and every operational change is recorded in an audit trail. Payments use idempotency keys and night audit runs in a background worker. Performance fixes took global search from 8.3 s to 0.45 s and sustained load from 10–25 to 50 requests per second with 0 errors (P95 192 ms, single instance).
