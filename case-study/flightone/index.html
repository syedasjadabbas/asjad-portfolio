FlightOne

An AI travel platform where Ava, the AI travel consultant, takes a traveller from one sentence to a booking, now connected to real airline and hotel suppliers.

Role Full-Stack & AI Engineer
Category AI Travel Platform · Client Project
Timeline 2026 · Updated Nov 2026
Core Stack Next.js · React · Express · Prisma · PostgreSQL · Gemini

Problem & Challenge

Travel booking is usually split across chats, spreadsheets and supplier portals, and AI is often a chat window bolted onto a website.

FlightOne is an AI travel operating system built around Ava, a travel consultant who turns a request like "Lahore to Karachi on 10 Oct" into live options, a checkout and support after the trip is booked. The hard part was making that work against real suppliers, where prices change and bookings can fail.

Architecture

One API serves every client, and every supplier sits behind the same adapter.

FlightOne Request Path
Web app and mobile app
Travellers ask Ava, compare options and book
↓
Express API
Ava, pricing, bookings, payments and servicing, across 29 server modules
↓
Supplier layer
A global airline booking system and an international hotel supplier, behind one adapter with a circuit breaker and search cache
↓
PostgreSQL through Prisma
Bookings, supplier quote snapshots and payment records

Key Decisions

1. The browser never sets the price

When a traveller picks a fare, the server runs the supplier search again, matches that exact offer and writes the quote itself. If the fare is gone, the booking stops with a clear message instead of swapping in another flight.

2. Margin stays internal

FlightOne's supplier cost and margin are stripped, at any depth, from every customer and company response. Staff reporting reads them through its own endpoints.

3. Money as integers

Every amount is a whole number in the currency's smallest unit, with an explicit currency code. There are no floats in the pricing path.

4. One Ava for web and mobile

Ava moved from the website into the API, so the website and the mobile app share the same assistant. Each Gemini attempt is capped at 15 seconds, and after an overload Ava skips Gemini for 60 seconds instead of failing in front of a traveller.

Supplier Integration

Flights: search, a live price re-check and seat holds, tested in the supplier's pre-production environment.

Hotels: search, booking and cancellation. All 7 certification test cases are complete, and certification is in progress.

Beyond Booking

The API also covers corporate travel (approvals, expenses, invoices and carbon reporting), group travel and MICE, visa, rewards, refunds and changes, journey tracking and escalation to human staff.

Status

The mobile app is live. Supplier booking launches after certification.
